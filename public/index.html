GOOD TO KNOW

A few quick answers

Do I need to know how to code already?

No. Beginner mode starts with guided steps and clear explanations. You can move to a harder level whenever you feel ready.

How long does a lesson take?

The daily AI spark is designed for about five minutes. The optional creator quest has six lessons of roughly ten minutes each.

Where does my progress go?

Daily lessons, mini-games, and practice days are saved in this browser. Google sign-in connects your learning journal to the configured learning record.

Are the articles written by LeArNCoDiNg?

The reading shelf gives short study summaries and links to the original work from established education and research organizations. Open each source to read or try the full resource.

03 · LEARN TOGETHER🤝

A beginner-friendly community lesson

Use a short activity to help a friend or a group explore coding. Ask a parent, guardian, teacher, librarian, or club leader before organizing a session.

  1. 5 min · Pick a question. For example: “What can a tiny dataset tell us about plant growth?”
  2. 10 min · Try the lab. Change a setting, read the bars, and explain what the sample shows.
  3. 5 min · Make a change. Ask learners to predict what will happen before changing a value.
  4. 5 min · Reflect together. What was clear? What would make the activity easier to use?
Make it welcomingOffer written and spoken directions, read labels aloud, use more than color to show meaning, and let everyone work at their own pace.

This is a starter plan, not a claim that a school, library, or club is partnered with LeArNCoDiNg.

04 · REFLECT WITH CARE✍️

Notice the learning impact

If you share an activity, reflect honestly on what you did and what you learned from other people.

  • Who was the activity designed to help?
  • What did learners find easy or confusing?
  • What did you change to make it more accessible?
  • What would you improve next time?

Describe only experiences that really happened. Never add names or private details about learners.
