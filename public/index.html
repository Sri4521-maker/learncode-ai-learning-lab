GOOD TO KNOW

A few quick answers

Do I need to know how to code already?

No. Beginner mode starts with guided steps and clear explanations. You can move to a harder level whenever you feel ready.

How long does a lesson take?

The daily AI spark is designed for about five minutes. The optional creator quest has six lessons of roughly ten minutes each.

Where does my progress go?

Daily lessons, mini-games, and practice days are saved in this browser. Google sign-in connects your learning journal to the configured learning record.

Are the articles written by LearnCode?

The reading shelf gives short study summaries and links to the original work from established education and research organizations. Open each source to read or try the full resource.
